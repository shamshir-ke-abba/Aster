# ASTER — Design Source of Truth

## 1. Product Identity

**Name:** Aster

**Core metaphor:** A private constellation of people.

Aster is a private messaging application designed primarily for a small, trusted group of friends.

The interface should feel like a small corner of the internet belonging to its users — not like a corporate communication platform.

### Core feeling

- Personal
- Private
- Calm
- Modern
- Social
- Atmospheric
- Slightly mysterious
- Lightweight
- Friendly without being childish

> The people are the focus. The constellation is the language.

---

## 2. What Aster Is NOT

Aster must not visually become:

- A WhatsApp clone
- A Telegram clone
- A Discord clone
- A Slack clone
- A generic chat application
- A cyber-security/hacker interface
- A cyberpunk neon interface
- A crypto/Web3 interface
- A children's messaging application
- A dashboard overloaded with cards
- An interface dominated by gradients

Avoid:

- Excessive neon
- Hacker-green aesthetics
- Giant lock/security imagery
- Generic speech-bubble logos
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Excessive blur
- Excessive rounded cards
- Excessive floating buttons
- Constant animations
- Gratuitous star decorations

Security is a technical property, not the visual identity.

---

## 3. Brand Metaphor — The Constellation

Every user is a point of light.

Every conversation is a connection.

A group is a constellation.

Aster is the space in which those relationships exist.

The metaphor must remain subtle. Aster should not become a literal astronomy simulator.

## 4. Color System

### Aster Night
`#080A12`

Primary application background. Almost-black with a subtle blue character.

### Aster Surface
`#111522`

Conversation surfaces, sheets and secondary containers.

### Aster Elevated
`#181C2A`

Menus, dialogs and selected states.

### Aster Violet
`#8B8DFF`

Primary interactive and branding accent.

### Aster Glow
`#B8B9FF`

Subtle highlights and atmospheric effects.

### Aster Star
`#F3F1FF`

Stars, high-emphasis text and tiny highlights.

### Aster Warm
`#FFDFA3`

Used extremely sparingly for special states.

---

## 5. Typography

Preferred direction:

- Inter
- Manrope
- Geist-style geometric sans-serif

Typography should be clean and highly readable.

ASTER branding may use increased letter spacing:

`A S T E R`

Avoid overly decorative fonts.

---

## 6. Logo

The Aster logo must NOT be a speech bubble.

The core symbol is a custom star.

The star should subtly suggest the letter A without becoming an obvious literal A.

Primary icon concept:

    ✦

The final logo should be geometrically simple enough to work at very small sizes.

---

## 7. App Icon

Dark background with a single luminous Aster star.

Concept:

    ┌─────────────┐
    │             │
    │      ✦      │
    │             │
    └─────────────┘

No text. No speech bubble. No lock. No complicated constellation artwork.

---

## 8. Home Screen

The home screen prioritizes conversations.

Example:

    Good evening, Saad

             ✦ Aster

    Your constellation
    ────────────────────────

    ✦ Alex                  2
      You coming tonight?

    · Rohan
      Send me that picture

    ✦ Sam                   4
      😂😂😂

    · Everyone
      Movie tomorrow?

Possible time-based greetings:

- Morning: Good morning
- Afternoon: Good afternoon
- Evening: Good evening
- Night: Still awake?

Do not make this gimmicky.

---

## 9. People as Stars

Users may be represented by subtle points of light.

Online: `✦`

Offline: `·`

The representation remains secondary to the person's name and conversation.

---

## 10. Group Identity

Group chats may use multiple points around a central point:

             ✦
          ✦  ●  ✦
             ✦

This is a visual language rather than a literal physics simulation.

---

## 11. Chat Screen

Chat remains extremely clean.

Concept:

             ←   Alex       ✦

    ─────────────────────────

                          Hey

                  Yeah I'm coming 😂

                         Nice

                  What time?

    ─────────────────────────
    +     Message...          ✦

Do not use giant colored message bubbles.

Messages should feel lightweight and integrated into the environment.

---

## 12. Animation

Aster should feel quietly alive.

Animations must be:

- Short
- Smooth
- Purposeful
- Low-amplitude
- Non-distracting

Avoid bouncing UI, long splash sequences, constant particle effects and animation on every interaction.

### Launch

    dark screen
        ↓
    tiny star
        ↓
    star gently brightens
        ↓
    ASTER
        ↓
    home

Target duration: approximately 500–700 ms.

---

## 13. Accessibility

Aster must support readable text sizes, sufficient contrast, large touch targets, reduced motion, screen readers and clear focus states.

Reduced Motion should substantially reduce decorative animation while preserving usability.

---

## 14. Source-of-Truth Rule

This document is the canonical visual authority for Aster.

If an implementation conflicts with this document, the implementation must be reviewed.

New design decisions should update this document rather than creating contradictory local conventions.
